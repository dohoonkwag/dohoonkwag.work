# The Summer Ahead
I'm back! It's already been almost a year since I've made this website, and there is still only one post... 

This summer, I want to be much more active here! I'm writing this blog post for a few reasons: to remind myself how to write using Markdown (lol) and to lay out everything I want to accomplish this summer (and therefore hold myself accountable for actually doing them).

Electrical and Computer Engineering Majors at Rice University are required to choose between AI & Systems, Computer Engineering, Neuroengineering, and Photonics, Electronics, and Nano-devices as an area of specialization. After learing about this, I did a bit of research into each area to figure out which specialization fits best with my interests and passions. After reading *The Brain That Changes Itself* by Norman Doidge and falling in love with neuroscience and neuroengineering, it makes the most sense for me to do the Neuroengineering specialization. 

So with a career in neuroengineering in mind, I want to use this summer to put myself in the best possible position to succeed in this career path. Here's what I plan on doing!
- **Signals and Systems Review**: I've realized through my coursework this year just how crucial this topic is, so I plan on rigorously working through *Signals & Systems* by Oppenheim, Willsky, and Nawab and posting my work here on this small corner of the internet! It took so long for me to grasp this content so I hope that what I am able to produce this summer can eventually help someone grasp it better/quicker.
- **Machine Learning**: While I do have a little bit of experience with machine learning, I don't feel as confident as I feel I ought to. So, in addition to *Signals & Systems*, I will be working through *An Introduction to Statistical Learning* by Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Rob Tibshirani, and Jonathan Taylor. 
- **Random Signals**: To prepare for my upcoming coursework on Random Signals, I'll be working through the textbook *A First Course in
Probability* by Sheldon Ross!
- **Neuromechanics Research**: I'm really excited to be working in a Neuromechanics lab over the summer, and I hope to be able to learn a ton of new things and post some of them onto here.
- **Passion Projects**: I have so many projects I want to make this summer, but I mostly focused on two: doing a neuroengineering passion project using the **Neurosky Mindwave Mobile 2** and **making my own iPod**. Many more details on these to come on these!

I am super excited to learn so much and build really cool things this summer, and I hope that documenting all of this will help someone down the line!
