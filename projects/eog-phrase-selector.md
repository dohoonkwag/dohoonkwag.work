# EOG Phrase Selector

<div class="project-tags">
    <span class="tag-pill">NeuroSky MindWave Mobile 2</span>

    <span class="tag-pill">HC-05</span>

Communicating using only eye blinks! The EOG Phrase Selector picks up
electrooculography (EOG) signals from blinks and eye movements and translates
them into phrase selections — no hands, no voice, just eyes.
